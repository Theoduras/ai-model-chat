Read this before connecting an account. This drives a real Discord account, not a bot application. Discord's terms do not allow that, and accounts found doing it are terminated — sometimes along with other accounts on the same device. Use an account you can afford to lose, keep her to servers she was actually invited to, and keep the chime-in rate low.

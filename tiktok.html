Read this before connecting an account. This drives a real TikTok account through a real sign-in, not TikTok's official Content Posting API — that one needs a developer app that passes a separate audit, posts privately until it does, and cannot reply to a comment at all. TikTok's terms do not allow an automated client either, and accounts found doing this can be actioned. Use an account you can afford to lose. Everything here stays safe for work: TikTok bars pointing anyone at adult content, so captions and replies never name a paid site.

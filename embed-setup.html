Two ways to share a persona chat: a plain link you can drop anywhere (bio, link-tree, socials), or a floating bubble you paste into a page you own. Both open the same persona chat, clearly labelled as AI. Both are for places you control — not a way to run this chat inside another platform's own inbox (a dating app's messages, for example), which that platform's terms wouldn't allow.
Direct link
Opens straight into a full-page chat with this persona — no picker, no other personas visible. Safe to hand out on its own.
Embed on your page
A floating "Chat with my AI companion" bubble instead of a full page — paste this on a page you own.
Paste this on your page
Drop it anywhere in the page's HTML, ideally just before </body>.

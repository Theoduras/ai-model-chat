One click. You get a personal link on our bot — put it in your bio, and anyone who opens it lands in a private chat with your persona. Nothing to install, no token to manage.

Open in Telegram
  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather and send /newbot.
  2. Give it a display name, then a username ending in bot.
  3. BotFather replies with a token like 7123456789:AAH… — paste it below.
  4. Optional: send /setuserpic and /setdescription to BotFather to brand the bot as the persona.
  5. Click Connect — the webhook is registered for you automatically.

🔗 Webhook
Registered automatically on connect. The path is unguessable and every request is checked against a per-bot secret header, so nobody else can puppet your persona.
Local development has no public HTTPS URL, so the webhook is skipped — deploy, or point the base URL at a tunnel, to receive messages.
👤 Real account — no “bot” label, no /start
Signs in as an actual Telegram account instead of a bot. It shows up as a person, fans can message it without pressing START, and it can open conversations itself. Needs its own phone number.
⚠ Automating a user account is against the spirit of Telegram’s terms. Accounts that message people who haven’t written first get rate-limited or banned — use a number you can afford to lose, and keep the volume low.
